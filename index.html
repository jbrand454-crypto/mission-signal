Mission: Signal

G7 · Topic 2 · Lesson 5 — Controlling Processes

Teacher Notes

Lesson: Savvas Elevate Science Gr. 7, Topic 2, Lesson 5 “Controlling Processes” (MS-LS1-8). About 35–45 minutes. Students can work alone or in pairs.

From the lesson slides (textbook pp. 118–121):

  • Stimulus and response: the diver and sharks (Connect It)
  • Neurons: dendrites, axon, axon tips; sensory neurons, interneurons, motor neurons
  • Nerve impulses; synapse (the signal changes from electrical to chemical and back)
  • Path of an impulse: dendrite → axon → axon tip → synapse → dendrite
  • CNS (brain + spinal cord) and PNS (nerves that branch out to the rest of the body)
  • Cerebrum, cerebellum, brain stem; what happens if the brain stem is damaged
  • Hands-on Lab: “Test how your knee responds to an external stimulus”

Added beyond the slides Beyond the slides (basic, well-established science):

  • Station 4 – Reflexes: the reflex arc (knee → spinal cord → muscle, with the brain told afterward). The slides list reflex only as a vocabulary word and mention the knee lab. The reaction times are rough figures (knee reflex about 0.05 s; a choice reaction about 0.2–0.3 s), and the animation is in slow motion.
  • Station 5 – Endocrine system: glands release hormones into the blood; the pancreas releases insulin and glucagon to keep blood sugar in a normal range; negative feedback. The slides list this section only in the outline (pp. 124–126) and gland and negative feedback as vocabulary. The blood sugar numbers are a simplified model, not real medical values.
